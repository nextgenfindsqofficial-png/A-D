Our Heritage

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted with Passion

At A&D FOODS, we bring age-old Sri Lankan culinary traditions straight to your table. Founded with a deep passion for authentic home-cooked flavors, every dish we prepare honors the rich heritage of Sri Lankan spices, hand-pounded coconut sambols, and slow-simmered curries.

From our signature morning Idiyappam presses to our aromatic Kiribath and custom catering spreads, we use only premium quality spices and farm-fresh ingredients to recreate the warmth and taste of home.
